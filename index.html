GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you open the console.

Have a different question? Start with a project brief.

What is SKYFORGE?

SKYFORGE is a cloud company profile concept, bringing compute, storage and managed services into one clear experience. This website includes an interactive console preview.

Can I try the console without signing up?

Yes. Open the console preview to switch between resources, choose a region and run a simulated deployment. The preview does not create servers or charge your account.

How does the pricing work?

The plans show illustrative monthly pricing in Indonesian rupiah. Annual billing applies a 20% discount. The annual total is shown when you select yearly billing; no payment is collected here.

Can I bring my existing application?

The proposed platform supports familiar Linux, container and database workflows. Use the solution explorer to see an example architecture for your workload.

Where are the regions located?

Jakarta, Singapore and Tokyo are included as example regions. Region status, latency and monitoring values on this site are sample data, not measurements of a live network.

How do I contact the team?

Use the project brief form to describe your requirements. In this company profile preview, the brief is saved only in your browser and can be downloaded as a text file.
